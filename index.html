Listening through touch

For me, the quality of a massage comes from presence and sensitivity: tuning in to you, physically and energetically. I talk mostly through touch and read the response of your body, so the session follows what you need on that day.

Muscle tension often softens together with the nervous system. Most people leave feeling calmer, lighter and more at home in their body. Sometimes emotions rise and release by themselves as tension lets go. That is welcome when it happens, and never the goal.

Trigger point work

Focused pressure on tight spots that hold pain and stiffness.

Myofascial release

Slow, sustained work with the connective tissue around the muscles.

Classic relaxing massage

Flowing Swedish strokes that settle the body and mind.

Thai and functional elements

Stretching and movement-based techniques, used when they help.
